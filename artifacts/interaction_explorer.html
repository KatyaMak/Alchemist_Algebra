The Interaction Explorer

Some effects only exist in the presence of another

In a simple model, each variable contributes independently to the outcome. In an interaction model, the effect of one variable depends on the value of another. Adjust the coefficients and watch how the prediction surface changes shape.

The Model

With β₃ = 0, the lines are parallel: X₁'s effect is the same regardless of X₂.

Parallel lines in the left panel mean no interaction. When lines diverge or cross, the interaction term is doing real work — the effect of one variable genuinely depends on the other.

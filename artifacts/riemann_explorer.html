The traveler wanted to know the speed of the river at a single stone. He moved his measuring sticks closer and closer together, watching the number settle — seeing the destination without ever arriving at it. Riemann did the same thing with area. The rectangles are not wrong. They are right in a limit. As n → ∞, the approximation becomes the exact truth — not by accident, but because that is the only value the sum can approach.

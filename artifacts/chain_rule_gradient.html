Two machines
The inner machine g transforms x first. The outer machine f receives g's output. The chain rule multiplies their individual sensitivities — how much g responds to x, times how much f responds to g.

The chain rule walking downhill
The gradient is computed using the chain rule — decomposing dL/dx into its parts. Gradient descent is just that derivative evaluated at each step, then subtracted from the current position. This is what Karpathy built. This is what backpropagation is.

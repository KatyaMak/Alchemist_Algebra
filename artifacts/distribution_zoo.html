The Central Gathering Point

All paths in the zoo lead here. The Central Limit Theorem declares that the sum of many independent random variables — regardless of their individual distribution — converges to the Normal. Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson, Geometric: given enough repetition, their averages find their way home to the bell curve. The zoo has one center of gravity. Everything converges.
